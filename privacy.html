Privacy policy

Prepared for FrameIsle 1.0.0 · October 30, 2026

Your library stays on your device

FrameIsle has no developer-operated account, advertising or analytics service. Connections, favorites, queues and playback history are stored locally. NAS passwords are stored in the system Keychain. Folder access uses permission granted through the system file picker.

Connections you choose

The app sends requests and, when needed, credentials to the NAS or WebDAV server you configure. That server can see connection information such as your IP address and requested paths. It is not operated by FrameIsle. HTTP connections are unencrypted; HTTPS certificates are validated. Local file providers may use their own services and policies.

Local processing and removal

Video and subtitles are processed for playback. Temporary subtitle files may be created locally. Removing a connection removes its saved credentials, favorites and associated playback records. System backups and Keychain behavior are controlled by your device and Apple settings. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee that operating-system backups or Keychain items are deleted.

Purchases and support

Apple processes purchases. FrameIsle does not receive payment card details. If you email support, your message and address are handled through the support email provider to respond to your request; do not include passwords or private media. Request deletion of support correspondence at otsgame@yahoo.com, subject to necessary legal retention.

Website and third parties

This site includes no analytics, advertising scripts or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process IP addresses and ordinary access logs to serve pages. Apple may process store, TestFlight and diagnostic information under its own settings and policies. Third-party library notices are available on the open-source page and inside the app.
