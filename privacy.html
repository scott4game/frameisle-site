Privacy policy

FrameIsle 1.0.0 · November 1, 2026

Your library stays on your device

FrameIsle has no developer-operated account, advertising or analytics service. Connections, favorites, queues and playback history are stored locally. NAS passwords are stored in the system Keychain. Folder access uses permission granted through the system file picker.

Connections you choose

The app sends requests and, when needed, credentials to the NAS or WebDAV server you configure. That server can see connection information such as your IP address and requested paths. Ordinary user-configured NAS servers are not operated by FrameIsle. HTTP connections are unencrypted; HTTPS certificates are validated. Local file providers may use their own services and policies.

Local processing and removal

Video and subtitles are processed for playback. Temporary subtitle files may be created locally. Removing a connection removes its saved credentials, favorites and associated playback records. System backups and Keychain behavior are controlled by your device and Apple settings. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee that operating-system backups or Keychain items are deleted.

Purchases and support

Apple processes purchases. FrameIsle does not receive payment card details. If you email support, your message and address are handled through the support email provider to respond to your request; do not include passwords or private media. Request deletion of support correspondence at otsgame@yahoo.com, subject to necessary legal retention.

Website and third parties

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. The website currently loads Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page visits and performance. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses and access logs for delivery and security. This website measurement is separate from the FrameIsle app, which does not include an analytics SDK. Apple may process store, TestFlight and diagnostic information under its own settings and policies. Third-party library notices are available on the open-source page and inside the app.

Contact

otsgame@yahoo.com

Offline media and configuration codes

Downloads are stored in an app-owned directory excluded from system backup. Incomplete files are removed when interrupted; completed files remain until you delete them. Removing a connection does not automatically delete its completed downloads.

QR configuration sharing happens only when you choose it. Codes include the server details and username; a password is included only if you enable that option. Codes are not encrypted. The system share sheet sends the image to the recipient or destination you select. Imported passwords are saved in Keychain, not in the connection database. Camera and selected QR images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a FrameIsle service.

Optional review service

If you explicitly connect to the FrameIsle review demonstration, its Cloudflare hosting processes connection metadata and dedicated demonstration credentials to serve original sample files. It does not connect to your NAS or upload your media. This optional service is separate from normal playback of servers you configure. Application request logging is disabled; Cloudflare may process operational data under its own policies.
