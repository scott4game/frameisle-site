FrameIsle uses AMSMB2 4.0.3 and its bundled libsmb2.
AMSMB2 is embedded as a dynamic framework; libsmb2 is compiled into that framework.
Copyright notices and LGPL 2.1 text are included alongside this notice.

Exact source revisions, included component notices, build instructions and remaining
release checks are documented in release/compliance/NATIVE-AMSMB2.md in the project.
Upstream sources: https://github.com/amosavian/AMSMB2/tree/4.0.3

Verified immutable corresponding-source archive:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scott4game/frameisle-site/32b9ebe662846c8b6eb62b92edb0d429888f2db8/sources/AMSMB2-4.0.3/AMSMB2-4.0.3-corresponding-source.tar.gz
