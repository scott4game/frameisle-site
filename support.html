Support

Need a hand? Contact otsgame@yahoo.com. Include your app version, device, OS version and steps to reproduce. Do not send NAS passwords, private media or credentials.

Connect to a NAS

Choose SMB or WebDAV and enter the server address and account. SMB can discover shares without a folder path. If discovery is disabled, specify a share under Advanced Settings. Your device must be able to reach the server.

Open local videos

Add Local Folder and select a directory using the system picker. Download cloud files first. If access expires, grant folder access again. The Photos library is not a file-folder source.

Favorite a folder

Open a folder and tap its star, or use the folder context menu. Favorites appear in the library. Removing a connection also removes its favorites.

Full screen and rotation

The exit button remains available in full screen. Tap the picture to show playback controls. iPhone follows the video's display orientation; resizable iPad windows may require you to rotate the device.

Subtitles and compatibility

Use embedded subtitles or a same-name SRT file in the video folder. MP4, MKV and MOV are supported containers; playback depends on the video and audio codecs inside. DRM-protected media is not supported.

Background audio

On iPhone and iPad, start a video with an audio track before locking. Silent videos pause. Force-quitting the app stops playback. Mac sleep is not supported for continuous playback.

Purchase and refunds

iOS/iPadOS and Mac are separate one-time purchases. Apple handles payments and eligible refunds through reportaproblem.apple.com.
