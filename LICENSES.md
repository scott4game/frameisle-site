Public-release audit is incomplete. The shipped playback binaries contain a module description matching a GPL-marked source file. Closed-source public release is on hold pending resolution of the component licensing and corresponding-source/relinking obligations. The LGPL label on the wrapper alone is not presented as clearance for every embedded component. Charging for software and keeping its source closed are separate licensing questions.

Contact: [otsgame@yahoo.com](mailto:otsgame@yahoo.com). Full license texts are also accessible inside the app under Settings → About → Third-Party Components and Licenses.
