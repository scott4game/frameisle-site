Connect your library

Browse SMB shares automatically or connect over WebDAV. Your media stays on the server you choose.

Keep favorites close

Save NAS and local folders for a quick return. Keep your queue and playback history on your device.

Watch your way

Full-screen playback, seeking, audio tracks and subtitles. Landscape and portrait videos keep their proportions.

Planned launch price: US$2.99 per platform, paid once. iOS/iPadOS and Mac are separate purchases. Local prices may differ. No subscription or in-app purchases. Availability will vary by region.

No films or shows are provided. Play media you own or are authorized to access.

Play MP4, M4V and MOV, plus compatible MKV with H.264 or HEVC video. Audio support depends on your device; supported candidates include AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, ALAC and MP3. MKV files need a usable index for seeking. AVI, TS, WebM, DTS, TrueHD, bitmap subtitles and MKV Dolby Vision are not supported. SRT/VTT subtitles are supported; ASS/SSA is displayed as plain text without styling or effects. Picture in Picture does not show overlay subtitles. MKV AirPlay video is not supported.
